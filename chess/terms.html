4. Online Play (Friends & Play a Stranger)

Both modes connect two devices through Google Firebase to play a live game — Friends with someone you've shared a game code with, Play a Stranger with whoever the app matches you to. Neither has accounts, chat, or spectators; the only thing that passes between two devices in a game is chess moves.
